Compass: Testing and Inspection



On Board Diagnostic (OBD)
The Compass system has extensive On-board Diagnostic (OBD) capabilities.

In the event that malfunctions occur in the compass system, troubleshooting is performed using the VAS 5051 Vehicle Diagnostic Testing and Information System or VAS 5052 Vehicle Diagnostic and Service System in operating mode "Guided Fault Finding".
