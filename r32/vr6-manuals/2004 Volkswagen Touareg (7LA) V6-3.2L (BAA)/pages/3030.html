Battery Tester with Printer 5097 A






Battery Tester with Printer 5097 A

CAUTION!
Risk of injury. Observe warning notes and safety precautions. Refer to --> [ Battery Safety Precautions ] Battery Safety Precautions!

When using Battery Tester with Printer (VAS 5097 A), it is not necessary to disconnect or remove the battery.

When using Battery Tester with Printer (VAS 5097 A), batteries with

• 95 - 574 A cold cranking output according to IEC (International Engineering Consortium)

• 136 - 855 A cold cranking output according to EN/ SAE (Europaische Norm/ Standard of Automotive Engineers)

* Batteries with cold cranking output greater than 499A according to DIN, for example 520A, 580A or 600A, can be tested for the time being with the setting for 499 A according to DIN.

can be tested.

The batteries are tested by being loaded with current that corresponds to the starter current of a vehicle. Under this load, the battery is evaluated and the measured results are output through the printer.
