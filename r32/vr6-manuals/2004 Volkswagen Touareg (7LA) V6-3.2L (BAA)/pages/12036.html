Front Seat Belts






Front Seat Belts

• Removal and installation is described for the right-hand side of the vehicle. Removal and installation for the left-hand side is performed in the same manner.

Removing

CAUTION!
Observe safety precautions for belt tensioners --> [ Belt Tensioner Safety Precautions ] Belt Tensioner Safety Precautions.

- Move seat as far forward as possible.

- Disconnect batteries.

- Remove B-pillar trim --> [ B-Pillar Trim ] B-Pillar Trim.

- Remove sill panel strip --> [ Sill Panel Strip ] Service and Repair.

- Remove belt anchor --> [ Belt Anchor ] Belt Anchor.

- Remove bolt - 2 - (50 Nm) and remove belt inertia reel - 1 - from B-pillar.

CAUTION!
Electrostatic charges may result in involuntary deployment of the belt tensioner. Therefore the technician must discharge static electricity from the body before separating the ignition and ground wiring. This is done e.g. by briefly touching the body or the door striker or pin.

- Depending on vehicle equipment, disconnect belt inertia reels from wiring harnesses.

- Remove bolt - 4 - (50 Nm) and remove belt relay - 5 - from belt height adjuster.

- Pull seat belt together with belt relay - 5 - and belt anchor - 3 - through belt guide - 6 -.





Installing

- Perform the steps used for removal in reverse order.

- Connect battery ground (GND) strap.
