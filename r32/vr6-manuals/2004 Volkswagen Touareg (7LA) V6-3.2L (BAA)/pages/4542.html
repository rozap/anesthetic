Minimum Curing Time



Minimum Curing Time

The minimum curing time for newly bonded special components is 3 hours.

Minimum curing time is the time from applying the components up to vehicle use. During this time, the vehicle must stand on an even surface at room temperature (at least 15°C).

CAUTION: The vehicle is operationally ready only after the minimum curing time has elapsed.
