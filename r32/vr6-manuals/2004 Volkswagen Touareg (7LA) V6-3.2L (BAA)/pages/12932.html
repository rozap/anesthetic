Tire Pressure Monitoring System, Generation 2






Tire Pressure Sensor

Removing

- Remove tire pressure sensor - 1 -.





- Counter hold metal valve using retainer (for example 2 mm spiral bore) while doing so.

Installing

CAUTION!
• Clean the valve opening before installing the tire pressure sensors.

- Press tire pressure sensor - 1 - against wheel and tighten.





- Counter hold metal valve using retainer (for example 2 mm spiral bore) while doing so.
