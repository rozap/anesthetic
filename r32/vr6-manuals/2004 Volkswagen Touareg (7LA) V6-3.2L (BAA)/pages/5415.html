Paint Damage, Repairing and Adhesive Sealant, Removing From Window






Flush Bonded Windows


Paint Damage, Repairing

In case of paint damage, recreate paint structure.

Adhesive Sealant, Removing from Window

- It is recommended to use adhesive remover D 002 000 10 as a cleaning solution. Follow the safety precautions when using it.

CAUTION!
When cleaning from inside the vehicle, do not press out on the window that was just installed.

- Wipe down the paint surfaces with a clean cloth. Remove residual adhesive with adhesive remover D 002 000 10.

- Clean the plastic trim: let the adhesive sealant harden for approximately 1 hour and then peel it off.
