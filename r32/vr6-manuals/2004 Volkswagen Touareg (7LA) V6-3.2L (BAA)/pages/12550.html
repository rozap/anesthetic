Temperature Sensors


Left Rear Vent

Removing

- Remove rear left backrest.

- Raise floor cover in Left Rear Vent Temperature Sensor (G405) area.





- Disconnect connector at Left Rear Vent Temperature Sensor (G405), rotate sensor in - direction of arrow - and remove sensor from air channel.

Installing

Install in reverse order.
