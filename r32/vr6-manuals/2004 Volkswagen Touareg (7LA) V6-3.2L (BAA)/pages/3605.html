A/C Unit Positioning Motors






A/C Unit Positioning Motors

Before working on electrical system wiring, remove relevant fuse(s)

• After installing a new positioning motor, the "Basic Setting " function must be performed. Refer to --> [ Climatronic A/C System with Automatic Control, Checking and Adjusting ] Climatronic A/C System with Automatic Control, Checking and Adjusting.

• For the malfunction "adaptation limit exceeded", check connecting element at motor/door, there must be no play between the shaft of the actuator motor and the connecting element.
