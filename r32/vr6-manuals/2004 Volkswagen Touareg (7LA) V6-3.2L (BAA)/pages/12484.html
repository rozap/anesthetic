Repairing and Handling Refrigerant






Repairing and Handling Refrigerant

• Information on repairs in vehicles with a climate control system and on handling refrigerant can be found in ELSA in the repair information " Refrigerant R134a - Servicing". Refer to General, Technical Data.

• Information on tools for repairs in vehicles with climate control system can be found in ELSA in repair information "Refrigerant R134a - Servicing "Refer to General, Technical Data.

• Under certain conditions, the dryer cartridge should no longer be replaced each time the refrigerant circuit is opened.

CAUTION!
Do not bend or kink A/C lines excessively.

There is a piece of foil in the refrigerant lines which could be destroyed.

Refrigerant lines must not be bent beyond a radius less than R = 100 mm.

Further information:

•

• A data plate on the lock carrier indicates the refrigerant used and capacity.
