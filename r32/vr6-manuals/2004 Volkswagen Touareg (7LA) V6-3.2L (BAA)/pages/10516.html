Rear Lid Lock, Removing And Installing

Removing







- Remove rear lid trim
- Unclip operating rod -1- from lid lock -2-.
- Disconnect harness connector -4- and unscrew bolts -3-.
- Remove lid lock.

Installing
Installation is performed in the reverse order of removal.
- Tightening torque: Bolts -3- 25 Nm.
