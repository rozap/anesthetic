License Plate Lamp: Service and Repair






License Plate Lamp

Removing

- Switch off ignition, switch off all electrical consumers and remove ignition key.

- Remove mounting bolts - arrows - of Left License Plate Lamp (X4) and Right License Plate Lamp (X5).





- Remove Left License Plate Lamp (X4) and Right License Plate Lamp (X5).

- Disconnect harness connector of Left License Plate Lamp (X4) and Right License Plate Lamp (X5).

Installing

Install in reverse order of removal.
