Initial Inspection and Diagnostic Overview






High-Mount Brake Lamp

The Touareg Electrical System

DTC recognition and display:

Comfort system is equipped with On Board Diagnostics (OBD) which assists troubleshooting of additional brake lamps.

For troubleshooting, use Vehicle Diagnostic, Testing and Information System (VAS 5051A) in operating mode " Guided Fault Finding".

• LED failure in high-mount brake lamps (not USA and Canada):

• Individual LEDs (Light Emitting Diodes) in auxiliary brake lamps are combined into groups of up to four LEDs and are supplied with power as a group.

• The high-mount brake lamp is designed in such a way that it still meets the legal requirements of the ECE (Economic Commission for Europe) when one LED group fails. If another LED group fails, these legal requirements are no longer met.

• When one LED group fails, the intact LEDs are given a higher load, by which failure of other LED groups can be assumed in the near future.

• If more than four individual LEDs in auxiliary brake lamp have failed, the auxiliary brake lamp must be replaced (repair measure).
