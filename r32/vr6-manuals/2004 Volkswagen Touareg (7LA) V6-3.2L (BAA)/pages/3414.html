Assembly Overview



Power steering pump, 6-cylinder gasoline engine
There is no provision for servicing the power steering pump.
If complaints are received determine cause with the help of pressure tests and leakage test. If a malfunction exists replace power steering pump.

Note:
^ If reservoir fluid level is low then check steering system for leaks.
^ In the case of leaks at the line connections first tighten connections and wipe dry then recheck for leaks.
^ Pumps supplied as spare parts and are not filled with oil. They must be filled with hydraulic fluid G 002 000 and to rotate pump by hand, otherwise the pump may be damaged or be noisy during operation.
^ Type of fluid: Hydraulic oil G 002 000
