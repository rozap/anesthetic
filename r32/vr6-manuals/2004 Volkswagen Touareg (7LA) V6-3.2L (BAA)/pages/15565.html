Front Final Drive






Driveshaft Flange Bonded Rubber Bushing

Special tools, testers and auxiliary items required

• Thrust piece (VW 442)

• Sleeve (30 - 14)

• Alignment fixture (3301)

• Alignment fixture (3346)

• Alignment fixture (3347)

• Thrust pad (VW 512)

• Thrust piece (VW 554)

• Alignment fixture (3253)

• Alignment fixture (T10030)

Removing

- Pull out bonded rubber bushing.





It is important to grease spindle (3346/2) with molybdenum grease before using.

Installing

- Pull in bonded rubber bushing - A -.





Pull in bonded rubber bushing - A - only so far until dimension - a - is obtained.

- Installation depth for bonded rubber bushing near the driveshaft flange.





Dimension - a - = 47.5 mm
