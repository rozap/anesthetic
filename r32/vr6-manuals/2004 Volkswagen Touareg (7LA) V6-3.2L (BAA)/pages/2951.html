Pressure Leak Test






Pressure Leak Test

Special tools, testers and auxiliary items required

• Brake pressure gauge 0-250 bar (VAG 1310 A)

Test Prerequisites

Brake system (hydraulic unit, brake hoses, lines and calipers) operating properly and free of leaks.

- Remove the bleeder valve from one of the front brake calipers. Connect and bleed brake pressure gauge 0 - 250 bar (VAG 1310 A).

- Apply pressure to brake pedal until the gauge indicates a pressure of 50 bar. The pressure must not drop by more than 4 bar during the test period of 45 seconds. Replace master cylinder if pressure drops greatly.
