Lower A-Pillar Trim






Lower A-Pillar Trim

• For a better overview, the instrument panel is shown uninstalled.

Removing

- Pry trim - 1 - out of sill panel strip using wedge (T10039/1).

- Remove the two bolts - 2 -.

- Pull door sill strip out of mountings in door sill in A-pillar trim transition area.

- Pull center A-pillar trim - 3 - out of the locking tab.

Driver side only

- Remove hood release lever --> Body Exterior 55 Removal and Installation

All vehicles

- Pull lower A-pillar trim - 4 - in opposite direction of travel out of mountings and door seal molding.





Installing

- Perform the steps used for removal in reverse order.

• Before installing, inspect the securing clips for damage and replace if necessary.
