Fluid Type Specifications



Coolant Type

Type G 12 Plus according to TL "VW 774 F" (purple)

G12 Plus can be mixed with previous coolant additives G11 and G12 (red). Make sure that system is replenished only with G12 Plus (due to its positives properties)
