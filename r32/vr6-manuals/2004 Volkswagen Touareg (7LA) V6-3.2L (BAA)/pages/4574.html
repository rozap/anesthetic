Wheel Housing, Fender and Sill Panel Extensions

Cleaning Instructions

- Clean the outer panel with adhesive remover (D 002 000 10) just before installing the extension. Then remove dirt, grease, wax or other contaminants with plastic cleaner (D 195 850 A1).

• Always use a clean, lint-free towel.
