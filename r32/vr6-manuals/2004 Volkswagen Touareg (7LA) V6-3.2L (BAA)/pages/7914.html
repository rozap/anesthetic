Wiper Frame With Linkage and Windshield Wiper Motor, Removing






Windshield Wiper System

Wiper Frame with Linkage and Windshield Wiper Motor, Removing

• If a linkage with part number 7L0.955.601.B/ 602.B or higher is installed on a Touareg through MY 2007, the plenum chamber cover must be cut before installing --> [ Wiper Frame with Linkage, through 22.07 ]. Wiper Frame With Linkage, Through 22.07

- Remove hex socket head bolt M6 - 1 - and remove washer.





- Remove bolts - 1 -.





- Unclip wire retainer - 1 - from wiper frame.





- Disconnect connector at Windshield Wiper Motor (V).

CAUTION!
• Connector catch is difficult to access in Right-Hand Drive vehicles.

• Connector catches may be destroyed.

• Connector catches must not be loosened by force.

- Remove the M6 hex socket bolt - 2 - and the washers below it.

- Remove the wiper frame with linkage upward from the vehicle.

• If Control Module for wiper motor (J400) or Windshield Wiper Motor (V) is replaced, Control Module for wiper motor (J400) must be coded --> [ Wiper Motor Control Module, Coding ] Testing and Inspection.
