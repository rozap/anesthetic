Window Frame Trim






Window Frame Trim

Removing

- Remove lower tailgate trim --> [ Lower Tailgate Trim ] Lower Tailgate Trim

- Close tailgate and open rear window.

- Pull upper window frame trim - 1 - in direction opposite of travel out of mountings in tailgate.

- Pull left - 2 - and right - 3 - window frame trim inward out of mountings in tailgate.





Installing

- Perform the steps used for removal in reverse order.

• Before installing, check securing clips for damage and replace if necessary.
