Repairing Hoses






Repairing Hoses

A new repair concept has been developed for repairing washer system hoses. Various individual hose connectors, adaptors, Ethylene Propylene Diene Methylene (EPDM) rubber hoses and shrink tubing will be offered as replacement parts.

• The replacement parts are available both for the repair of a smooth tube as well as for the repair of a corrugated tube.
