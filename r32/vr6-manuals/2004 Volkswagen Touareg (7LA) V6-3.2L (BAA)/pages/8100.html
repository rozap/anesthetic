Windshield and Headlamp Washer Fluid Reservoir Filler Neck

Removing

- Before filler tube - 1 - of tank can be removed, front right fender must be removed





- Remove mounting bolt - arrow - for filler tube - 1 -.

- Turn filler tube - 1 - upward and remove it forward out of windshield and headlamp washer system reservoir.

Installing

Install in reverse order of removal.
