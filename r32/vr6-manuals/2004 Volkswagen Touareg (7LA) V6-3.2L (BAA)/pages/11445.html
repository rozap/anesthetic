Analog Tuner For Television Reception TV






Analog Tuner For Television Reception TV

The TV tuner is available as special equipment in the vehicles with the "Radio Navigation System 2" with multi-function display (MFD)

Analog television programs can be received with the TV tuner.

While driving, the television picture is darkened for safety reasons but the sound can still be heard. For additional information, --> Operating instructions.

The TV tuner is equipped with 2 input channels on which additional audio/video devices can be connected. These channels can be activated using the RNS 2 operating menu. Refer to --> Owner's Manual

Connecting additional devices to the TV. Refer to --> [ Connecting Additional Audio/Video Units on TV Tuner ] Connecting Additional Audio/Video Units on TV Tuner.

• Before troubleshooting or servicing, the technicians must be familiar with the function and operation of the radio unit/radio navigation system and of the TV tuner.

• When performing service work or Fault-Finding, use --> Vehicle Diagnosis, Testing and Information System (VAS 5051) or Vehicle Diagnosis and Service System (VAS 5052) in the function "Guided Fault Finding",.

• When the battery is reconnected, check any affected system or component (radio, clock, comfort electrical connection etc..) according to the repair information and/or the operating instructions.

Troubleshooting

TV tuner is capable of vehicle self-diagnosis.

When performing Fault-Finding, use Diagnostic Operation System (VAS 5051) or Vehicle Diagnosis and Service System (VAS 5052) in the function "Guided Fault Finding ".
