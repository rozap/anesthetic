Removing





- To remove trim - 5 -, turn cover - 7 - 90° and remove bolts - 6 -.

- Remove trim - 5 - in direction of arrow.

Installing

• Before installing, check all the fasteners for damage and replace them if necessary.

To install, perform the steps used for removal in reverse order.
