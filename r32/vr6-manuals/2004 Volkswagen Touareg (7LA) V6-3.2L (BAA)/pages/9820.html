Sunroof With Solar Panel Overview






Sunroof with Solar Panel


Operating without Electricity

- Remove the console from headliner.

- Insert the hex key into the drive motor bolt and press it upward - arrow -.





- The bolt can be pushed in a few millimeters - a -. The sunroof panel can only be moved in this position.

Water Drain Hoses, Cleaning

• For cleaning, an assisting tool is recommended, such as a speedometer driveshaft inner cable, approximately. 2300 mm (90.5 in.) long.

Front Water Drain Hoses





Front water drain hoses - 1 - run through the A-pillars and end at the engine compartment bulkhead. They are cleaned from the sunroof opening.

Rear Water Drain Hoses





The rear water drain hoses - 2 - run through the D-pillars and end in the rear wheel housings. Clean starting from the wheel housing and working outward. The wheel housing liner must be removed for this.
