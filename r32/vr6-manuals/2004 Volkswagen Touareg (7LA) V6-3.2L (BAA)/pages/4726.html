Exterior Rearview Mirror






Exterior Rearview Mirror

Removing

- Remove tweeter.

- Disconnect the exterior rearview mirror connectors - 1 - and disengage the wire on the subframe and door.





- Remove the bolts - 1 - and remove mirror.





Installing

To install, perform the steps used for removal in reverse order.
