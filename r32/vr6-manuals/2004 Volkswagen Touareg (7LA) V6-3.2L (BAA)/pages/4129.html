Entertainment Systems: Description and Operation






Multi Media System, Rear Seat Entertainment

• Each time there is a complaint with the multimedia system, ask the customer for the four-digit anti-theft code before starting a repair!

• Before troubleshooting or servicing, the technicians must be familiar with the function and operation of the Multi Media System.

• For additional information. Refer to --> Operating instructions.

• The multimedia system can be operated regardless of whether the ignition is switched on or off. However, when the ignition is switched off, it automatically shuts off after approximately 20 minutes.

• The remote control must be aimed at the DVD player when using because the receiver is installed there.

• When the battery is reconnected, check any affected system or component (radio, clock, comfort electrical connection etc..) according to the repair information and/or the operating instructions.

• Mini CDs (8 cm diameter) can only be played with a suitable adapter.

• CDs which are not round (so-called shape-CDs) must not be inserted, they may destroy the disc drive.

• DVD Plus cannot be used.

The multimedia system offers a variety of entertainment possibilities for the passengers on the rear seats.

It is equipped with a DVD player.

DVD films, video CDs in various formats and CDs with music in MP3 format can be played, for additional information. Refer to --> Owner's Manual. The sound is delivered via wireless headphones. Furthermore, additional equipment can be connected for playback of other visual and audio sources. Video and audio devices and consoles can be connected.

The multimedia system consists of the following components:

• a 7-inch liquid crystal monitor (TFT/LCD display) in roof console.

• a DVD player

• One remote control for multimedia system

• two sets of wireless headphones with infrared technology for the multimedia system (on some vehicles, wired headphones may be included instead of wireless ones)

• Connector sockets for various auxiliary devices in the roof console

Troubleshooting

• Always pay attention to notes and the compulsory registration before every repair on the Multi Media System,.

The Multimedia system is not equipped with self-diagnosis.

Repairing the multimedia system is not intended at this time. If repairs are needed, the system is completely removed and replaced with a new one.
