Air Bag(s) Arming and Disarming: Service and Repair


Single/Dual Battery Systems

When working on the airbag system, the vehicle batteries ground (GND) straps must be disconnected. No waiting time is necessary after disconnecting the batteries. When connecting the airbag system to a voltage source no person can be present inside the vehicle.

Steps for performing repairs on airbag system.

1. Disconnect batteries ground (GRD) straps.
2. After installing/repairing of components is completed, switch on ignition.
3. Close doors.
4. Connect batteries ground straps.

Visual and functional check of airbag warning light system.

- The warning light must light for approximately 3 seconds when the ignition is switched on.
- The warning light must switch off after approximately 3 seconds and then not light again or flicker during a road test.
