Oil Pressure Regulator Valve: Service and Repair






Oil Check Valve

Removing

- Screw approximately 4 threads of a commercially available bolt M8 x 1.5 into the valve - arrow - and pull it from the bore.





Installing

- Coat O-ring with oil before installing. Press the valve into the oil channel - arrow - by hand.
