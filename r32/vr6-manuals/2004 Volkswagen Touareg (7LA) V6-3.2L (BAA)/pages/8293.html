Exterior Mirror Turn Signal Lamps






Exterior Mirror Turn Signal Lamps

• No standard lamps are installed in mirror turn signals, rather long-lasting light-emitting diodes (LEDs).

• If damaged, the entire turn signal must be replaced.

• Removing and installing driver side lamp and passenger side lamp is performed in the same manner on both sides and is only described for one side.

Removing

- Switch off ignition, switch off all electrical consumers and remove ignition key.

- Removing lamp for mirror flasher: Exterior Mirror, through 10.2006, or Exterior Mirror. from 11.2006

Installing

- Installing mirror turn signal lamp: Exterior Mirror, through 10.2006, or Exterior Mirror. from 11.2006
