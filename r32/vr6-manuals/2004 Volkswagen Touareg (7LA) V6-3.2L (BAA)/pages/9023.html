Tire Pressure Monitoring System, Generation 2






Tire Pressure Monitoring System, Generation 2

A new generation of Tire Pressure Monitoring was used from MY 2008.

• The specified pressures are stored in the tire pressure monitoring control module (J502) and only need to be changed if the wheel dimensions have changed, e.g. if different summer or winter tires are installed. Refer to --> Owner's Manual. The system automatically learns new or different wheel electronics.
