Installation Notes

The surfaces of the components to be primed must be free of dirt, grease, wax and other contaminants.

CAUTION: Components must be installed within 10 minutes, otherwise bonding properties of adhesive will be impaired.

- Keep gap dimensions as narrow as possible.
- Secure components using adhesive tape during curing time.
