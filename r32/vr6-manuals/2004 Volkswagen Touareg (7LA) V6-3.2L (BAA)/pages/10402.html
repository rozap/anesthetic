Body Emblem: Description and Operation






Name Plate Installation Instructions

• Warm the name plate with a hot air gun (V.A.G 1416) before removing.

• Use only adhesive remover (D 002 000 10) to remove any remaining adhesive.

• Make sure the adhesive surfaces are free of dust and grease.

• Install the name plate immediately after cleaning.

• Peel off the protective film just before installing.

• The temperature must be approximately 70 °F (21 °C).
