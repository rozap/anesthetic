Selector Shaft Seal






Selector Shaft Seal

Special tools, testers and auxiliary items required

• Thrust piece (T10174)

• Scribe

- Be careful when using scribe to remove seals.

- Apply Automatic Transmission Fluid (ATF) to the circumference and space between the sealing lips of the new seal.

Installation position:

The open side of the sealing ring faces the transmission.

- Drive in the new seals using thrust piece (T10174), thereby not distorting the seal.

- Multi function Transmission Range (TR) switch, installing and adjusting. Refer to --> [ Multi Function Transmission Range Switch ] Multi Function Transmission Range Switch.
