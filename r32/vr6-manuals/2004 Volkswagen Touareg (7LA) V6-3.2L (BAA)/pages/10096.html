Rear Interior Locking Buttons

• Removal and installation of Front Passenger's Interior Locking Button (E309), Left Rear Interior Locking Button (E310) and Right Rear Interior Locking Button (E311) is performed in the same way and is only described for one switch --> [ Front Passenger Interior Locking Button ] Front Door Lamps and Switches.

• Switch Illumination Lamp (L156), which cannot be replaced separately, is integrated in Front Passenger's Interior Locking Button (E309).

• Switch Illumination Lamp (L156), which cannot be replaced separately, is integrated in Left Rear Interior Locking Button (E310).

• Switch Illumination Lamp (L156), which cannot be replaced separately, is integrated in Right Rear Interior Locking Button (E311).
