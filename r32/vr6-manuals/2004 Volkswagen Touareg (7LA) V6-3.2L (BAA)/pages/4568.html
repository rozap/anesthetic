Wheel Housing, Fender and Sill Panel Extensions


Rear Wheel Housing Extension Assembly Overview

• Removal and installation is described only for the left-hand side. Removing and installing on the right side is identical.





1 Rear door, wheel housing extension

• Material PUR - GF 17

• Adhered to the rear door.

2 Adhesion point

• dia. approximately 20 mm

• 2K adhesive (DA 004 600 A2)

3 Side panel wheel housing extension

• Material PUR - GF 17

• Adhered to side panel.

4 Wheel housing extension, bumper

• Material PUR - GF 17

• Adhered to the rear bumper cover.
