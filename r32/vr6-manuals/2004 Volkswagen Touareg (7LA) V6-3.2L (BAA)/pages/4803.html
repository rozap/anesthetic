Solar Contact in Solar Panel

Removing

- Sunroof Solar Panel

- Place solar panel on suitable surface, top face down to prevent damage.

- Remove bolt - 2 - and pull solar contact - 1 - out of guide in direction of arrow.





- Separate connector - 3 - from solar contact.

Installation

- To install, perform the steps used for removal in reverse order.

- Tightening specifications for bolts: 2 Nm.
