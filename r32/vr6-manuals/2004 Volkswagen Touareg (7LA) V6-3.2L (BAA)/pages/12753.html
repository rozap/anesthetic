Air Door Actuator / Motor: Description and Operation



A/C Flap Motors, General Information

CAUTION:
Before beginning repairs on the electrical system:
- Determine system or component fuse/fuses using applicable wiring diagram.
- Remove system or component fuse/fuses.

NOTE:
- Prior to motor removal, initiate "Basic setting in case of service".

- Following repairs and/or replacement of motors, initiate basic setting.


- If malfunction message "Adaptation limit exceeded" is present, check motor/flap connecting piece. There should be no play between motor shaft and connecting piece.
