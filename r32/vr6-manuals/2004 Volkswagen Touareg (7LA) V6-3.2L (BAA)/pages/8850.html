Rear Tire Pressure Monitoring System Antenna






Rear Tire Pressure Monitoring System Antenna

Removing

The rear tire pressure monitoring antenna are installed behind the wheel housing liners opposite the direction of travel.

- Switch ignition off.

- Remove wheel housing liner.

- Disconnect connector - 1 -.





- Remove the expanding rivets - arrows - and remove the rear tire pressure monitoring antenna.

Installing

- Insert the rear tire pressure monitoring antenna and secure it with expanding rivets - arrows -.





- Connect harness connector - 1 -.

- Install wheel housing liner.
