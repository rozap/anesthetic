Door Window






Front Door Windows


Door Window

Removing

- Remove front door trim.





- Disconnect harness connectors - 2 - from window regulator motor with control module - 1 -.

- Remove bolts - 3 - and remove window regulator motor with control module - 1 -.

- Remove the lock cylinder housing. Refer to --> [ Lock Cylinder Housing ] Front Door





- Unclip the clip - 1 - from the door handle - 2 -.

- Pry out cover caps - 1 - in - direction of arrow -.





- Remove the bolts - 3 - for window frame.

Tightening specification: 20 Nm

- Remove bolts - 2 - from door lock.

Tightening specification: 8 Nm

- Pry out cover caps - 1 - and - 5 -.





- Remove the bolts - 2 - and - 4 - for window frame.

Tightening specification: 20 Nm

- Disconnect boot - 3 - from A-pillar and disconnect harness connectors.

- Guide boot and connectors through opening in outer door panel.

- Remove all bolts - arrows -.

Tightening specification: 8 Nm





- Disconnect harness connector - 2 - and guide it through opening in assembly carrier.

- Pull front part of subframe slightly away from outer door panel - arrow a -.





- Be careful to guide the wiring as well.

- Pull subframe out of outer door panel toward hinge side - arrow b -.

- When doing this, make sure that door lock does not catch in outer door panel.

- Lower the door window by hand to lower stop.





- Remove both bolts - 1 - from window securing elements - 2 -.





- Fold open both securing elements - 2 -.

- Lift the door window - 3 - out of securing elements and pull the door window - 3 - downward out of window guide - 4 -.

Installation

• Rubber buffer - 3 - is supplied with new window regulator in a plastic bag.

- Before inserting the door window mounting elements - 4 - make sure that rubber buffers - 3 - are inserted into the window regulator mounts - 1 - - 2 -.





- Guide door window - 3 - into window guide - 4 - and place door window into securing elements - 2 -.





- Install the screws - 1 - and push door window to upper stop by hand.

- Press the door window - 1 - forcefully into upper corner of window guide - arrow - and tighten clamping bolts - 2 - (quantity: 2) (3.3 Nm).





Then, continue in reverse order of removal.

• A function test must be performed before door trim is installed.
