Left Front Tire Pressure Monitoring Transmitter






Left Front Tire Pressure Monitoring Transmitter

Removing

The left Front Tire Pressure Monitoring Transmitter (in wheel housing) (G431) is installed on the longitudinal member behind the wheel housing liner.

- Switch ignition off.

- Remove wheel housing liner.

- Separate connector - 1 -.





- Remove the Left Front Tire Pressure Monitoring Transmitter (in wheel housing) (G431) from the bracket - 2 -.

Installing

- Insert the Left Front Tire Pressure Monitoring Transmitter (in wheel housing) (G431) in the bracket - 2 -.





- Connect harness connector - 1 -.

- Install wheel housing liner.
