Rear Blower Regulation






Door Motors


Rear Blower Regulation

Removing

- Bring rear heating and air conditioning unit into service position. Refer to --> [ Second Rear Heating and A/C Unit, Service Position ] Second Rear Heating and A/C Unit, Service Position





- Disconnect connector - 1 - at Rear Blower Regulation Motor (Bitron) (V306).

- Lift tab - 2 - slightly and rotate Rear Blower Regulation Motor (Bitron) (V306) in - direction of arrow -.

- Remove the Rear Blower Regulation Motor (Bitron) (V306) from the heating and air conditioning unit

Installing

Install in reverse order.

• Condensation water hose to valve must not be kinked or pinched. Refer to --> [ Rear Condensation Water Hose with Valve, Checking ] Rear Condensation Water Hose with Valve, Checking.
