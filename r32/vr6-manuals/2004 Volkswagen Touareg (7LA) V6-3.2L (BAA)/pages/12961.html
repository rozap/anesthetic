Storing Current Tire Pressure, Generation 1






Storing Current Tire Pressure, Generation 1

All tires, spare tire included, must always be at the specified tire pressure.

For example, after repair work on a wheel/tire, the tire pressure of all the wheels/tires including the spare must be checked.

- Inflate all wheels/tires, spare tire included, to prescribed tire pressure.

- Enter current tire pressures in system via front information display control head.
