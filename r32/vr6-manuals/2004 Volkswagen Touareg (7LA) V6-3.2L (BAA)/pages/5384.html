Bolted Windows


Rear Lid Hinged Window, Adjusting

- Remove rear spoiler, refer to --> [ Rear Spoiler ] Rear Spoiler

- Loosen striker pin.





- Loosen the nuts - 3 - on the hinge - 2 - at left and right (do not remove).

- Align the rear lid hinged window - 1 - corresponding to the exterior vehicle contours.

- Tighten the nuts - 3 - after adjusting the rear lid hinged window.

Tightening specification: Bolts - 3 - 8 Nm
