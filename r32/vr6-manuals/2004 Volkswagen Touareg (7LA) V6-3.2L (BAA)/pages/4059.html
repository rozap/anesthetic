Telephone System






Telephone System

There are several possible versions of the telephone systems in the Touareg:

• as a complete telephone system with telephone cradle on instrument panel

• as a telephone preparation with telephone cradle on instrument panel

• as a telephone system with telephone cradle in center armrest

• as a telephone system with telephone cradle in center armrest and Bluetooth technology

• as a telephone preparation with telephone cradle in center armrest and Bluetooth technology

The telephone system or telephone preparation is only available in conjunction with a radio unit or radio navigation system.

It is possible to retrofit telephones (cell phones) in a vehicle with telephone preparation.

On vehicles with telephone preparation in which the telephone cradle is installed on the instrument panel, the telephone operating electronics control module (interface box) was already installed at the factory. Only a telephone cradle adapter for the mobile phone use is required (accessory).

Troubleshooting

The telephone system and telephone preparation are equipped with On Board Diagnostic (OBD).

When performing Fault-Finding, use Diagnostic Operation System (VAS 5051) or Vehicle Diagnosis and Service System (VAS 5052) in the function "Guided Fault Finding ".
