Preparing Bodywork For Adhesive



Preparing Bodywork For Adhesive

- Remove any adhesive residue with Adhesive Remover VAS 6349.
- Clean adhesion surface thoroughly with adhesive remover D 002 000 10 immediately prior to bonding on attachments.
- Adhesion surfaces must be free of dust and grease and there must be no residual adhesive present.

- Carry over the dimensions for the primer surface from the components onto the bodywork.
- Apply glass/paint primer D 009 200 02 uniformly in one stroke onto metal parts using applicator D 009 500 25.

When adhering to bumpers, use the plastic primer D 009 600.
