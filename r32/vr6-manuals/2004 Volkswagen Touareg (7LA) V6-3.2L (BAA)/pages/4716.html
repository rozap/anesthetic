Mirror Glass






Exterior Mirror, through 10.2006


Mirror Glass

CAUTION!
Wear protective eyewear and gloves when working.

Removing

- Protect the paintwork on the edge of the housing - 1 - with fabric-reinforced adhesive tape.





- Push mirror glass into mirror housing at bottom.

- Press off - arrow 5 - mirror glass - 3 - from retainer - 2 -, using pry lever 80-200.

- Swing mirror glass upward - arrow 6 - and disconnect connector - 4 - for mirror heater on back of mirror glass.

Installation

- Reconnect the mirror heating connector on the mirror glass.

- Press the center of the mirror glass onto the retainer in the housing.

- The mirror glass will engage with a click.
