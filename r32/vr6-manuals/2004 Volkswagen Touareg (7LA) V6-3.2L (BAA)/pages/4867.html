Front Seat Frame Trim






Front Seats

Front Seat Frame Trim

Removing

• Removal and installation is described for the left-hand side of the vehicle. Removal and installation for the right-hand side is performed in the same manner.

- Position seat as high and far back as possible.

- Remove trim - 1 - in rear area upward out of mounts in seat frame - 2 - - arrow A -.





- Remove trim in direction of travel out of lower mounts in seat frame - arrow B -.

Installing

- Perform the steps used for removal in reverse order.
