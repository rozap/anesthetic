Second Rear Heating and A/C Unit, Service Position






Second Rear Heating and A/C Unit, Service Position

- Remove side trim at left of luggage compartment.





- Remove heating and air conditioning unit - A - screws (8 Nm).

- Tip heating and air conditioning unit to right.

• Refrigerant lines and hoses remain connected.

• Observe notes concerning repair work on air conditioned vehicles and on handling refrigerants. Refer to--> [ Repairing and Handling Refrigerant ] Repairing and Handling Refrigerant

• Ensure correct seating of condensation water hose with valve after installing rear heating and air conditioning unit. Refer to--> [ Rear Condensation Water Hose with Valve, Checking ] Rear Condensation Water Hose with Valve, Checking
