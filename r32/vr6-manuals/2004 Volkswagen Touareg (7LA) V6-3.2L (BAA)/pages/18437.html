ATF Circuit, Bleeding






ATF Circuit, Bleeding

- Check and correct the Automatic Transmission Fluid (ATF) level. Refer to --> [ ATF Level Checking and Filling ] ATF Level Checking and Filling.

CAUTION!
Wear protective glasses.

- Perform road test. ATF temperature must reach at least 100 °C to vent the ATF circuit.

When the air leaves the ATF circuit, the ATF level in the transmission decreases. Therefore, the ATF level must be checked again later after the fluid cools.

- Cool ATF to below 30 °C.

- Check ATF level again. Refer to --> [ ATF Level Checking and Filling ] ATF Level Checking and Filling.
