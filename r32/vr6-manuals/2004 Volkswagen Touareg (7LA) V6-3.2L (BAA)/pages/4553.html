Door Moldings Assembly Overview

• The illustration shows the left side. It can also be applied to the right side.

• The door moldings are attached at the factory with double-sided adhesive tape.

• It is not possible to remove the door moldings without destroying them.

• Further information on wheel housing, fender and sill panel extensions can be obtained via Volkswagen Technical Helpline.
