Door






Front Door


Door





- Remove the boot - 3 - from the A-pillar. Separate connectors - 2 -.

- Remove hex nut - 1 - from hinge pin. Tightening specification: 30 Nm.

- Remove the lower bolt - 5 - from the hinge. Tightening specification: 20 Nm + 1/4 turn (90°) further

• Loosened hinge bolts must always be replaced.

- Lift the door up out of the hinge.
