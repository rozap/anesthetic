Paint Damage, Repairing



Paint Damage, Repairing

For paint damage in visible areas and for severely damaged points in the non-visible area of removed window, paint structure must be repaired.

The following repair method applies to minor paint damage in an area of removed window which is not visible: Apply two coats of glass/paint primer D 009 200 02 over each other (wet on wet) in opposite directions.

- Apply once over damaged area.
- Air dry for 10 minutes.
- Apply a second coat but apply it in the opposite direction as the first one.
