Anti-Theft Alarm System

--> Owner's Manual

General Description:

Anti-theft alarm system functions are integrated in Comfort System Central Control Module (J393).

The most important information for activating and deactivating is provided by Access/Start Authorization Control Module (J518). Communication occurs via Comfort CAN-Bus.

Interior Monitoring Deactivation Switch (E267) and Deactivate Vehicle Inclination Sensor Button (E360), removing and installing --> [ Interior Monitoring and Vehicle Inclination Deactivation Switch, through
11.06 ] Interior Monitoring and Vehicle Inclination Deactivation Switch, through 11.06.

DTC recognition and display:

Anti-theft alarm system is equipped with On Board Diagnostics (OBD) which assists troubleshooting.

For troubleshooting, use Vehicle Diagnostic, Testing and Information System (VAS 5051A) in operating mode " Guided Fault Finding".
