Windshield Washer Spray Nozzle: Adjustments






Windshield Washer System Spray Nozzles, Adjusting

CAUTION!
• Danger of damage.

• Washer nozzle may be damaged.

• Do not use solid objects to clean the spray nozzles!

• If contamination in the spray nozzle causes an uneven spray pattern, remove the nozzle and rinse it with water in the opposite direction of the spray. It is permissible to further blow through in opposite direction of spray with compressed air. Do not use solid objects to clean the spray nozzles!
