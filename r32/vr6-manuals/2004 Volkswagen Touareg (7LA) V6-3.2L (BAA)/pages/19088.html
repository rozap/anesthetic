Vehicle Level Sensors, Removing And Installing
Level sensors that are part of the adaptive suspension system transmits the vehicle attitude in the form of signals to the headlight range control modules. This signal is determined by the adaptive suspension control module on the basis of signal input from all vehicle level senders.

The vehicle level senders are part of the adaptive suspension system. Refer to Ride Height Sensor, Suspension Control.
