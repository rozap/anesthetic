Front Housing Bonded Rubber Bushing






Front Housing Bonded Rubber Bushing

Removing

- Pull out bonded rubber bushing.





It is important to grease spindle (3346/2) with molybdenum grease before using.

- To do so, place special tool (3346/1) with flat shoulder facing toward bonded rubber bushing onto the spindle (3346/2).

Installing

- Pull in bonded rubber bushing - A - until it is centered in front final drive housing.
