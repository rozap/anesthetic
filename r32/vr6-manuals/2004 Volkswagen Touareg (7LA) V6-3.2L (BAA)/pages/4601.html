Center Console






Center Console

Removing

- Set parking brake.

- Select rearmost driving range position.

- Switch off ignition.

- Remove center console storage compartment --> [ Center Console Storage Compartment ] Center Console Storage Compartment

- Remove console ashtray --> [ Console Ashtray ] Console Ashtray

- Pry trim - 1 - out of locking tabs using wedge (T10039/1), beginning at the lower section.

- Remove four bolts - 2 -.

- Take vent unit - 3 - out of center console and separate connectors.





- Remove the two bolts - 1 -.

- Remove the two bolts - 2 -.

• Only one screw - 2 - is installed.

- Remove four bolts - 3 -.

- Raise center console and separate connectors as necessary, depending on vehicle equipment.

- Remove center console - 4 - from vehicle.





Installing

- Perform the steps used for removal in reverse order.
