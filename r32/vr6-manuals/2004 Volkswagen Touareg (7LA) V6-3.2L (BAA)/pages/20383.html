Condenser Fan Motor Relay: Locations




Coolant Fan (FC) Control Modules J293 and J671

The coolant/condenser fan(s) for this vehicle is/are controlled by the Coolant Fan (FC) Control Modules J293 and J671 and does not use relays.

For the Coolant Fan (FC) Control Modules J293/J671 locations, please see Relays and Modules - Cooling System/Radiator Cooling Fan Control Module/Locations, Locations
