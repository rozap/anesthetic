Rear Final Drive (08D,09D Transmission)






Transfer Case Transporting

Special tools, testers and auxiliary items required

• Adapter (10 - 222 A /7)

• Engine sling (2024 A)

- Attach the engine sling (2024A) as follows to the lifting eyes of the transfer case and transport using a workshop crane.





Input shaft side:

4. Hole for hook in position 1 of the engine sling - arrow A -.

Output shaft side (driveshaft flange):

2. Hole for hook in position 8 of the engine sling - arrow B -.

CAUTION!
The hooks and locating pins must be secured with locking pins.
