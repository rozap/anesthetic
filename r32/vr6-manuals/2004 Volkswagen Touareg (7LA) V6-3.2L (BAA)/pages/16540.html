Fuel Pressure Release: Service Precautions




WARNING!
- The fuel or fuel lines in fuel system can become very hot (danger of scalding)!
- In addition, the fuel system is under pressure! Before opening the system, place rags around the connection area and release pressure by care fully loosening the connection!
- Wear protective goggles and protective gloves when working on the fuel system!
