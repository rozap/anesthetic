Ash Tray: Service and Repair






Console Ashtray

Removing

- Select rearmost driving range position.

- Switch off ignition.

- Remove center console storage compartment --> [ Center Console Storage Compartment ] Center Console Storage Compartment

- Remove shifter cover --> [ Shifter Cover ] Shifter Cover

- Open ashtray unit - 1 -.





- Detach ashtray unit by pulling backward out of mountings in center console.

- Close ashtray unit.

- Remove ashtray unit from center console.

- Disconnect ashtray unit from wiring harness.

• Instead of ashtray unit, a storage unit with electrical socket may be installed. Removal is identical.

Installing

- Perform the steps used for removal in reverse order.
