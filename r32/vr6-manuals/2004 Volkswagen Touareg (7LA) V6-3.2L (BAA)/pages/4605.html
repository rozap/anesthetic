Shifter Cover






Shifter Cover

Removing

- Switch off ignition.

- Remove center console storage compartment --> [ Center Console Storage Compartment ] Center Console Storage Compartment

- Remove the two bolts - 1 - (1.4 Nm).





- Remove cover - 2 - upward out of mounts in center console.

- Disconnect all connectors from cover.

Installing

- Perform the steps used for removal in reverse order.
