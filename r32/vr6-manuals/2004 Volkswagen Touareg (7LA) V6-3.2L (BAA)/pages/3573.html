Tire, Mounting






Tire, Mounting

• Make sure wheel is clean.

- Insert a new tire valve.

- Remove valve core.

- Inflate tire to approximately 3 to 4 bar, tire bead must audibly slide over wheel hump.

- Install valve core.

- Correct inflation pressure to specifications.

- Balance wheel.
