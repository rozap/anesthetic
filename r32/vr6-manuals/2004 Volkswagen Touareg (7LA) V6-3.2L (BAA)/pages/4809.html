Sunroof with Solar Panel


Solar Panel, Checking Parallel Movement

- Open the sunroof about 10 cm.

- Pull the trim - 2 - in the center - arrow - out of the mount - 1 -.





- Move solar panel to zero position.

- Push the trim back.

Guide pin must align with marking - arrow - on left and right guide rails.





If this is not the case, adjust parallel movement, refer to --> [ Solar Panel, Adjusting Parallel Movement ] Solar Panel, Adjusting Parallel Movement
