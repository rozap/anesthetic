Rear Lid Electronics, Basic Setting

Use the vehicle diagnosis, testing and information system (VAS 5052) to perform a basic setting on the rear lid electronics.

- Select "Guided Fault Finding". in vehicle diagnosis, testing and information system (VAS 5052)

- Using the "Go to" button, select "Function/Component selection" and the following menu items one after the other:

• Body (Repair Group 01; 27; 50 through 97)

• Body - Repair work (Repair Groups 01 and 50 to 77)

• 01 - On Board Diagnostic (OBD) capable systems

• Rear lid cover electronics

• Functions - rear lid cover electronics

• Rear lid cover electronics - basic setting
