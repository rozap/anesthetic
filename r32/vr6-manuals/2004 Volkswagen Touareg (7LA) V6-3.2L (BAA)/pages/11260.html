Speaker: Locations






Speaker System Overview





1 - Navigation System Antenna (R50) and Radio/Telephone Antenna (R51)

• installed in right front wheel housing above headlamp

• additional information in chapter, antenna systems, refer to --> [ Antenna Systems ] Antenna Systems

2 - Radio (R) or Radio/Navigation Display Control Module (J503) (radio navigation system)

• additional information in Delta radio chapter, refer to --> [ Delta Radio System ] Delta Radio System or RNS 2 chapter, refer to --> [ Radio Navigation System 2 with MFD ] Radio Navigation System 2 with MFD

3 - Antenna Selection Control Module (J515)

• installed under rear of headliner

• additional information in chapter, antenna systems, refer to --> [ Antenna Systems ] Antenna Systems

4 - Antenna Amplifier 2 (R111) and (R112)

• installed in upper part of rear lid

• additional information in chapter, antenna systems, refer to --> [ Antenna Systems ] Antenna Systems

5 - Digital Sound System Control Module (J525)

• with 8-channel analog technology

• installed in right of luggage compartment

• additional information in "Sound 1" and "Sound 2" sound systems amplifier chapter, refer to --> [ CD Changer ] Description and Operation

6 - CD Changer (R41)

• installed in right of luggage compartment

• additional information in CD changer chapter, refer to --> [ CD Changer ] Description and Operation

7 - Left Rear Midrange Speaker (R105) and Right Rear Midrange Speaker (R106)

• Diameter 60 mm

• Impedance 4 ohms

• installed in rear doors near door opener

• Removing and installing, refer to --> [ Rear Mid-Range Speaker ] Speakers

8 - Left Rear Bass Speaker (R15) and Right Rear Bass Speaker (R17)

• Diameter 168 mm

• Impedance 2 ohms

• installed in bottom of rear doors

• Removing and installing, refer to --> [ Bass Speaker, Rear ] Speakers

9 - Left Front Bass Speaker (R21) and Right Front Bass Speaker (R23)

• Diameter 220 mm

• Impedance 2 ohms

• installed in bottom of front doors

• Removing and installing, refer to --> [ Front Bass Speakers ] Speakers

10 - Left Front Midrange Speaker (R103) and Right Front Midrange Speaker (R104)

• Diameter 80 mm

• Impedance 4 ohms

• installed in front doors near door opener

• Removing and installing, refer to --> [ Front Mid-Range Speakers ] Speakers

11 - Left Front Treble Speaker (R20) and Right Front Treble Speaker (R22)

• Diameter 46 mm

• Impedance 4 ohms

• installed in front doors in triangular mirror trim

• Removing and installing, refer to --> [ Front Treble Speaker ] Speakers

12 - Center Mid/High Range Loudspeaker (R158)

• only installed in conjunction with sound system amplifier

• installed in center of instrument panel

• Diameter 60 mm

• Impedance 4 ohms

• Removing and installing, refer to --> [ Center Speaker ] Speakers
