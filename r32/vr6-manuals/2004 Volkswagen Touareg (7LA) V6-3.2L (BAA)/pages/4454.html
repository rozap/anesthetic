Removing





- Harness connectors of installed electrical components and hoses must be disconnected.

- Loosen nuts - 2 - (do not unscrew).

- A second mechanic is required for the rest of the removal procedure.

- Remove the gas-filled struts - 8 - from the hood. Refer to --> [ Gas Strut ] Gas Strut

- Only now remove hex nuts - 2 - and remove rear hood - 1 -.

Installation

To install, perform the steps used for removal in reverse order.

- Hood, Adjusting, refer to --> [ Hood, Adjusting ] Hood Overview
