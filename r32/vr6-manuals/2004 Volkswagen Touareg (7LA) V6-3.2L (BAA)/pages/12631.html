Climate Control Exhaust Gas Sensor: Service and Repair






Air Quality Sensor

Removing

- Remove plenum chamber cover, left.

- Lift plenum chamber cover up slightly on left side.

- Disconnect connector - 1 -.





- Disengage Air Quality Sensor (G238) - 1 - from bracket at plenum chamber cover - arrow -





Installing

- Install in reverse order of removal.
