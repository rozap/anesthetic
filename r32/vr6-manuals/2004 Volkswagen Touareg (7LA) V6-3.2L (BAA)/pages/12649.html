Front Blower Regulation Motor






Front Blower Regulation Motor

Removing

CAUTION!
The fan wheel can become deformed.

Setting the front blower regulation motor (Bitron) down incorrectly causes the fan wheel to become unbalanced.

Set the front blower regulation motor (Bitron) down so the fan wheel is not covered.



- Remove footwell trim on front passenger side.

- Remove bolts - 1 -.





- Disconnect connector - A - from Front Blower Regulation Motor (Bitron) (V305)

Installing

CAUTION!
The fan wheel can become deformed.

Setting the front blower regulation motor (Bitron) down incorrectly causes the fan wheel to become unbalanced.

Set the front blower regulation motor (Bitron) down so the fan wheel is not covered.



- Install in reverse order of removal.
