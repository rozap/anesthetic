Procedures






Tires, Mounting

As of start of production for the Touareg, new wheels have been installed on this vehicle.

For mounting tires, the workshop mounting device must be equipped with the tire mounting fixture intended for these wheels.

Otherwise, there is a danger of damaging the wheel.

If the tire mounting device has not yet been modified, contact the device manufacturer.

After mounting tire to vehicle, tire pressure must be stored. Refer to --> [ Storing Current Tire Pressure, Generation 1 ] Storing Current Tire Pressure, Generation 1.
