Right Seat Backrest






Rear Seats


Right Seat Backrest

Removing

- Fold down rear backrests.

- Remove bolt - 1 - from backrest mounting - 5 - (16 Nm) and remove clip - 2 -.

- Remove bolt - 3 - (50 Nm) and remove seat belt anchor - 4 -.

• The bolt is micro-encapsulated and must be replaced every time it is loosened.

• Before new bolts are installed, the thread of the corresponding nut must be cleaned.

- Remove right-hand backrest upward out of backrest mounting - arrow A - and pull off of pin - 6 - - arrow B -.

CAUTION!
To avoid damaging pins, backrest should be folded forward approximately 45° when removing.





Installing

- Perform the steps used for removal in reverse order.
