Resetting the Tire Pressure Monitoring System



If a "CHECK TIRE PRESSURES", or "FAULTY WHEEL IN VEHICLE" warning light is observed, check and inflate the tire(s) to specified pressures then store new pressure values as follows to shut light off:

- In the "TIRE PRESSURE" menu accessed from the main convenience setup menu, select "STORE". Current tire pressures are saved as new recommended pressures.
