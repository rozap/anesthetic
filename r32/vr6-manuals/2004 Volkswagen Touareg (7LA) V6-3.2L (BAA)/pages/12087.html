Malfunction Lamp / Indicator: Testing and Inspection



Visual and functional check of airbag warning light system.

- The warning light must light for approximately 3 seconds when the ignition is switched on.
- The warning light must switch off after 3 seconds and then not light again or flicker during a road test.
